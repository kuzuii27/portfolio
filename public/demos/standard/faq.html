Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before your visit.

Do you accept HMO?

Yes: Maxicare, Intellicare and Medicard. Bring your HMO card and a valid ID.

How much is a cleaning?

₱1,000 - ₱1,500 depending on how much tartar there is (sample price).

Are you open on Sundays?

No. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but sending an inquiry or calling first means less waiting.

Does cleaning hurt?

Most patients feel only mild pressure. Tell your dentist if you have sensitive teeth.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available near the clinic (sample answer).

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3 and up.

HMOs we accept

  • Maxicare
  • Intellicare
  • Medicard
